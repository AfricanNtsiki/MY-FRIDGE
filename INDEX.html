FAQ

Questions?
We've got answers.

Here are some of the most common questions about renting a MyFridge.

How much does a MyFridge cost?

Our example student rental starts from R300 per month. The final price depends on the fridge selected and the applicable rental terms.

Do I have to buy the fridge?

No. MyFridge is designed around renting, allowing you to use a fridge without purchasing the appliance outright.

Who can rent a fridge?

MyFridge is primarily designed for students and people living in accommodation where a personal fridge is useful.

How do I request a fridge?

Complete the rental enquiry form above and provide your contact and accommodation information.

Can I contact MyFridge before renting?

Yes. Use the enquiry form to send us your questions before making a rental decision.
